In Accounting, Dividend Income bearing securities, bonus shares, right shares and right issue cases. There is final dividend and interim dividend. Final dividend is declared in annual general meeting and is issued after the current financial year. While interim dividend is issued on the decisions of the board of directors anytime during the financial year. According to PARA 13 of AS13, a member can sell their rights to another individual to exercise that right... this is almost like acting like a broker... we're the middle men but offering the share at a discount from market price. 

Few silly things to remember, Selling the rights do not get recorded in investment a/c. Stamp duty is only given on purchase. Obviously, if government takes it twice, no one would buy shares.
