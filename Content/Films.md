[The Awakenings](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0099077/)
its a good movie 4/5, the title sounds like a horror film but its not. its a good slow paced, feel good film based on a true story, can watch it with family.

[Due Date](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt1231583/) 
Absolutely enjoyed it. Its fast paced and Humorous. Worth a one time watch. 

[War Dogs](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt2005151/) 
haaaaaahahahaahahaaaaa its a FUN movie about two friends and how they together start a arms distribution business. Believe me it sounds serious but nothing like that, when you see [Johan Hill laughing](https://youtu.be/waUJLgWaD5k?si=tO7MdHCP0Tzdd7Dc) i bet you will pause the movie and recreate the laugh . 

[The Station Agent](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0340377/) Just Perfect. If someone wants to learn film making this has to be on the list. Shot in 20 days with a very small budget

[Killers of the Flower Moon](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt5537002/) Based on a true story. About how a bunch of white Americans murdered the people of osage tribe for their oil money. But it's very long, and would need to do some research or rewatch to understand every little detail if you have no idea about the American history. 

[Enthiran](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt1305797/) This has to be the best Indian cinema in the Pre-Bahubali era. The VFX, Sound Design, Sets, Dialogues, Animatronics, Music, Story... everything is just so great for 2010. But I guess the audience at that time did not appreciate it. I watched it in the theatre when i was may be 7 or 8 years old i guess. 

[PK](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt2338151) It's a fun yet also being a thought provoking movie for sure. This film would not pass the censor board if it were to release today. 

[One Battle After Another](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt30144839) Funny at times, a few "What?!!!" moments here and there... its good. 

[Bugonia](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt12300742/) Some characters remind me of the idiots I avoid in real life, and the idiots I occasionally have to become just to survive

[Ford vs Ferrari](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt1950186/?ref_=tt_mlt_i_11) I'm not a fan for cars but this movie is something special, i loved christian bale's character. Jaw dropped when i learned it was based on true story. The movie's ending made me sad.
